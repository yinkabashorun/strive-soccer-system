## Caption (draft)

Your team trains the team. Nobody is training you.

So the hours you put in alone decide everything, and most players spend them on a bit of everything. Twelve drills a week across six things is two drills each. That is why your weak foot is the same foot it was three months ago.

Put eight of those twelve on one thing for two weeks. Make week two harder than week one. Keep one drill a session for the rest of your game so nothing goes cold. Then move to the next thing.

That is how I came back from six months out with a hip tear. It is how Coach Gary and I build every Strive Elite player's three months.

DM ELITE and tell us the one thing you'd fix first.
