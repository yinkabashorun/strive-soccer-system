# 5v5 captains: start your team's club in the app

Decided Nov 26 2026 (Coach Yinka: "lets go with your advice"): each captain
starts their own club in the Strive Elite app and sends its code to their
roster, so the captain lets their own players in and nothing waits on a
coach. Send this once the TestFlight public link exists and build 10 is the
build behind it (the door that asks for a code is in build 10).

Draft, his to send. The button names are the app's own.

## The text to captains

> Captains, every 5v5 team gets its own club in the Strive Elite app. Setting yours up takes two minutes.
>
> 1. Get the app: [the TestFlight public link]
> 2. Tap Join free and make your account.
> 3. Open Clubs, tap Start a club, and name it after your team.
> 4. Tap Invite a teammate and send it to your roster. They type your club code when the app asks for a code.
> 5. When a player asks to join, open your club, tap Manage, and let them in.
>
> From there your whole roster is on one board. Start a training in the app and your time counts for your club every week.
>
> Text me back if anything gets stuck.

## What a player gets from their captain (the app writes this when the captain taps Invite a teammate)

It carries the club's code and a link. On an iPhone the link opens a page
with the code and "Get the app" once the public link is saved on /coach/free.
