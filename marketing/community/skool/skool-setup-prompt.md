# Prompt for the Claude Chrome extension: set up the Strive Skool community

Paste everything between the lines into the Claude extension in Chrome,
signed in to skool.com on a laptop. The two image files it will ask for
are next to this file: skool-icon-1024.png (group icon) and
skool-cover-1920x1080.png (group cover). Have them on the laptop's
desktop before you start.

---

You are setting up a Skool community for Strive Soccer on skool.com, in
the browser I am signed in to. I am Coach Yinka, the owner. Another
Claude session already made every decision below with me; your job is
to carry them out on the site, exactly as written, and to ask me only
the questions listed under ASK FIRST. If anything on the site does not
match what is written here, or a step needs something not covered
here, stop and ask me. I will relay your question and come back with
the answer.

HARD RULES
- Never enter card details, change the plan, or confirm any payment
  without asking me first and getting a yes in this chat.
- Never invite anyone, import contacts, or send any email or DM from
  the community. Invites come later.
- Never write the words "AI", "automated", "algorithm" or "bot"
  anywhere in the community. Never use an em dash. Say "your player",
  never "your child" or "your kid". Where the copy speaks for the
  coaches it is "we" (Coach Yinka and Coach Gary), not "I".
- Never add a player's name, a former player's name, a college or a
  club as proof anywhere. No testimonials, no alumni lists.
- Do not create a classroom course, upload any drill, or add any
  training content. Training lives only at thestriveapp.com.
- Copy the text blocks below character for character. Do not improve
  them.

ASK FIRST (ask these in one message, wait for my answers, then start)
1. Group name: Strive Elite. Confirm, or give another.
2. The URL slug. Default: strive-elite (so the link is
   skool.com/strive-elite). If taken, try strive, then tell me what is
   available and let me pick.
3. Plan: the Hobby plan ($9 a month) unless I say otherwise. Tell me
   before the billing step and wait.
4. Who else gets admin or moderator: nobody for now unless I name
   someone. (Hobby may allow one admin only; if the site says so, tell
   me.)
5. The Monday Call: day, time (Eastern) and the Zoom link. If I do not
   have them yet, skip the calendar step and tell me it was skipped.

SETUP, IN ORDER

1. Create the group with the name and slug from ASK FIRST.

2. Icon and cover. Upload skool-icon-1024.png as the group icon and
   skool-cover-1920x1080.png as the cover image. If the site asks for a
   different size, tell me the size it wants instead of cropping.

3. Privacy and joining. Private group. Free to join. Membership
   requests require approval. If there is a setting for who can see the
   group in search or discovery, set it to the most private option
   available.

4. About page / description. Paste this, exactly:

Strive Elite players train at home with a week built around them, every
Monday, by Coach Yinka and Coach Gary. The training lives in the app at
thestriveapp.com. This is where the players and their families meet.

Post your clips, ask the coaches anything, see what the other players
are working on, and show up for the calls. Keep it about the work.

Membership is free for current and former Strive families.

5. Membership questions (the questions a person answers when they
   request to join). Add these three, in this order, all required:
   - Player's first and last name
   - Player's age and current club or team
   - Your name and how you are related to the player (parent, guardian,
     or the player)

6. Rules. If the group has a rules or guidelines section, add these
   four lines, one per rule:
   - Full sentences, real names, no screenshots of other people's
     messages.
   - Players, parents and coaches only. No selling anything.
   - Post your own clips only, and only your own player.
   - Nothing about another player's team, playing time or coach.

7. Categories for posts. If the site lets you set post categories,
   create exactly these, in this order: General, Clips, Calls, Wins.
   Delete any default category that is not on this list.

8. Pinned welcome post. Create a post in General titled
   "Start here" with this body, exactly, then pin it:

Welcome to Strive Elite. Everything you train is in the app:
thestriveapp.com. Sign in there every Monday for the new week.

Here, three things. Post your clips in Clips so we can see the work.
Check Calls for the next call and the link. Put the wins in Wins, big
or small.

Questions about your player's plan go to us in the app chat, not here.

9. Calendar. Only if I gave you the day, time and link in ASK FIRST:
   create a recurring weekly event named "The Monday Call" (or the
   weekday I gave you, if it is not Monday), Eastern time, with the Zoom
   link as the location, and this description, exactly:

Weekly call for Strive Elite players and parents. Bring one thing from this
week's training. Link above.

10. Classroom. Leave it empty. If the site created a sample course or
    module by default, delete it.

11. Notifications. If there is a group-level setting for member
    notifications or a digest email, leave the defaults.

12. Owner profile. If my own Skool profile has no photo, upload
    skool-icon-1024.png there too. Set my display name to "Coach Yinka"
    if the site allows it. Do not change my email.

WHEN DONE
Send me, in one message: the group's link, the plan the group is on,
which steps you completed, which you skipped and why, and anything the
site asked for that was not in this list. Do not invite anyone.
