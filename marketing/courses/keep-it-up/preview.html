Keep It Up

The Juggling Ladder

Seven levels, easiest to hardest. Every drill has a pass mark. Hit it, move up.

How it works
  1. Start at Level 1, even if you can already juggle.
  2. Pass the catch version, then work the keep it up version, then move up.
  3. A pass mark is in a row. A drop ends the attempt.
  4. Film the day you pass a level.
1

One touch

Beginner

One touch, catch

Cue: toe up, ankle locked, hit the middle of the ball.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

One touch, keep it up

Cue: small touches, both feet.

2

Two touches each foot

Beginner

Two touches each foot, catch

Cue: the first touch is small, the second one sends it to your chest for the catch.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Two touches each foot, keep it up

Cue: small touches, stay on the balls of your feet.

3

One, two

Beginner

One, two, catch

Strong footthenWeak footthenWeak foot

Cue: say it as you go, strong, weak, weak, catch. The weak foot gets two in a row, so give its first touch a little more time.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

One, two, keep it up

Strong footthenWeak footthenWeak foot

Cue: keep the count going out loud until the weak foot stops thinking.

4

Laces, inside

Beginner

Laces, inside, catch

Cue: the laces pop it up, the inside brings it to your chest.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Laces, inside, keep it up

Cue: turn the foot for the inside touch, then straight back to the laces.

5

Thigh

Beginner

Drop, thigh, catch

Cue: knee up, thigh flat like a table, ball lands on the middle.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Thighs high, keep it up

Cue: drive the knee, catch it on the flat of the thigh, wait for it to come down.

6

Thighs, laces

Beginner

Thighs, laces, catch

Cue: thigh flat like a table, then meet the ball with the laces on the way down.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Intermediate

Laces, thigh, catch

Cue: the laces touch is not soft, it gets the ball to chest level. The thigh is the softer touch, it controls it.

Advanced

Thighs, laces, keep it up

Cue: the thigh sets the height, the laces keep the rhythm. Stay tall.

7

Head

Advanced

Head it up

Cue: eyes open, forehead not the top of the head, knees soft.
