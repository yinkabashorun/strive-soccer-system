# The six courses, Trim and Stack format (Oct 31 2026)

Coach Yinka, Oct 29: "let's get a list of the courses and list them like
this", with page 91 of $100M Offers (Trim and Stack): Problem, Solution
wording, Sexier name for the bundle, then the actual delivery vehicles.
Every delivery line below is live in the app today. Values use the
deck's rule: one private session at Strive's confirmed $80 rate to be
taught the course. The per-level alternative is at the bottom, unstamped.

---

**Juggling** -> He can't keep it up past five touches and dreads the
warm-up circle -> How any player can juggle with feet, thighs and head in
a few yards -> **Keep It Up**, the juggling ladder ($80)

- a) Seven levels, easiest first. Pass the catch version, then the keep
  it up version, then move up.
- b) Fourteen filmed drills, every clip under ten seconds, one cue each.
- c) A pass mark on every beginner drill, ten in a row.
- d) When the coach offers it, the next rung lands in the player's week
  right after the warm-up and rotates up the ladder.
- e) Once a rung is in the week, Send a Clip on that card and a coach
  replies in the app.

**Weak foot** -> His left foot is for standing on, and it's not moving at
practice -> How any player can trust the weak foot under pressure, with
or without a wall -> **Weak Foot** ($80)

- a) Four levels: touches, off the ground, wall moves, wall turns.
- b) Eight filmed drills. Levels 1 and 2 need no wall.
- c) Weak foot is a rated pillar; the coach's rating on the progress
  page moves as the weeks go.
- d) The week builder places weak foot drills when that is the player's
  gap, so the course and the week work the same muscle.

**Strength and injuries** -> He gets knocked off the ball, and I worry
about injuries -> How any player can build core, hips and legs with no
equipment -> **Strength** ($80)

- a) Five levels: Core A, Core B, Hips and glutes, Legs and groin, Power.
- b) Seventeen filmed drills, bodyweight only, a few yards.
- c) The finisher rule: when a coach note or the player's check-in
  mentions an injury or prevention, every session ends with one.
- d) Otherwise up to two finishers a week, never counted as a skill
  drill.

**Speed** -> He's a step slow to every ball -> How any player can get
faster on their own in an open space -> **Speed** ($80)

- a) Five levels: warm-up, technique, acceleration, top speed,
  explosiveness.
- b) Seventeen filmed drills, three plyos and fourteen sprints.
- c) The plyos already run as the week's warm-up rotation.
- d) Sprint drills go into a player's week only when the coach offers
  them.

**College recruiting** -> Nobody at the club will tell us how college
recruiting works, and the emails never get answered -> How any family can
run the college route themselves with the right email ready every time
-> **College Recruiting** ($80)

- a) Eleven parts, twenty-four pages, each part one job: grades, the CV,
  film, the school list, outreach, exposure, the visit, money, the
  decision, the timeline.
- b) The one-page CV template.
- c) Five email templates: the first email, before a game, after a game,
  the follow-up, after a camp or visit, each with its rules of thumb.
- d) The tape rules and the full game.
- e) Twenty schools across three levels, and how to read a roster for
  position need.
- f) ID camps, showcases and the money test before any check is written.
- g) Unofficial visits and what to ask a coach, the player asking.
- h) Money plainly, how to decide, and the tracker.
- i) "Ask us" wherever the rules change by year; the question goes to a
  coach in the app chat.

**Academy recruiting** -> He's good at his club but we don't know how MLS
NEXT or an academy actually finds a kid -> How any family can climb the
academy ladder one rung at a time -> **Academy Recruiting** ($80)

- a) Twelve parts, twenty pages: the ladder, your league, the club coach
  and the director, the CV, film, outreach, the tryout, age and grades,
  the pro academy, overseas, the rules, the parent's job.
- b) The CV by birth year, the way academies read it.
- c) The email to a director and the game-day, follow-up and
  after-tryout emails, all by birth year.
- d) One tape a season from league games, the full game underneath.
- e) The tryout: before, the first ten minutes, after.
- f) How the MLS academies take players and what to ask when an offer
  comes, in writing.
- g) The overseas page: no money up front, nothing under 18.
- h) The tracker by club, and the parent's job on the far side of the
  field.
