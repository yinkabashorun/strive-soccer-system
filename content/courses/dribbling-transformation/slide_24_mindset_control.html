Control

Ball mastery is not about tricks. It is about control — having the ball do exactly what you intend, every single time, under pressure, at speed.

Control is what allows you to play simple when the game demands it, and complex when the moment calls for it. Without control, skill is just showboating. With it, you become dangerous.

"Control the ball.
Control the game."
— Andres Iniesta
