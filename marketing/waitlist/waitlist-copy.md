# The Strive app waitlist: copy (draft, Nov 22 2026)

Everything here is a draft for Coach Yinka. Nothing is posted or sent.
The waitlist link is thestriveapp.com/join (add ?src=ig, ?src=5v5 or
?src=pickup so the coach page shows where each person came from).

The three perks, his (Nov 22): pickup sign-up 48 hours before anyone
else; a 5v5 team with its whole roster on the list gets its own graphic
in the social rollout and starts in the app as a club; standouts at the
5v5 who are on the list get the first Strive Stars. No opening date and
no spots number appear anywhere below, on purpose: none is confirmed.

## 1. The captain text (5v5 teams, sent by Coach Yinka or Carla)

Hey [Captain first name], one thing for the 5v5. The Strive app is
opening soon and there's a list. Get on it here: thestriveapp.com/join?src=5v5
Then send your link to every player on your roster. If your whole roster
is on the list, your team gets its own graphic in the 5v5 rollout and
starts in the app as a club on day one. Let's get your team seen.

## 2. The pickup announcement (posted 48 hours AFTER the list gets the link)

Story or post, after the list has had its 48 hours:

PICKUP SIGN-UP IS OPEN.
The list had it 48 hours ago.
Next time, be first. Get on the list: link in bio.

The text the list gets (sent from /coach/free, "Pickup sign-up, the list
first"):

[First], Strive pickup sign-up is open to the list first. You have 48
hours before we post it anywhere else: [link] Lock your spot. Reply STOP
to stop these texts.

The app's opening soon. Get on the list here: thestriveapp.com/join?src=ig
You get pickup sign-up 48 hours before we post it, and if you bring 3

## Rules held

Players are 13 and older (the list refuses anyone younger). No em
dashes, no "AI", no invented dates or spot counts. A 5v5 roster is up to
9 players and a club holds 20, so every full roster fits as one club.
