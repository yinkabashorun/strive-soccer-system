# Strive Elite offer stack: intake (Coach Yinka's additions, verbatim intent)

Captured Oct 12 2026 as Coach Yinka said them. Nothing here is built or
decided beyond what he said. Each item maps to a problem from his list
(marketing/offer/strive-elite-problem-list.md and the solutions list).
His framing for the whole stack: low effort for him, high real and
perceived value, and each one doubles as a pre-objection handler.
Naming comes later, Hormozi-style. "This is the most important project
we've ever had."

## 1. Training load plans (from the previous message)
- Problem: scared of being overworked (and the mirror: four sessions
  feels like underworked).
- What he said: the current 4 x 40 minute sessions become the busy
  schedule plan ("train less with more"). Add a new layer of extra
  training for players with more in the tank: full session templates
  for the off season that don't need to be rebuilt.
- My counter on the table: load as a player setting (In-Season / Off-
  Season), Extra Work pulled from the bank on demand or coach-authored
  sets of bank drills, never a static template outside the system.
- Open: sessions and length for Off-Season; who sets the load; on-demand
  vs coach-authored vs both; calendar or manual switch.

## 2. Accountability (player side): will my player do the training?
- Problem: accountability.
- What he said: weekly mindset / accountability Zoom calls; access to a
  Skool community "of winners"; the weekly texted check-ins from the app.
  The app texts must NOT feel AI-like and must not do too much: Skool and
  the Zooms carry accountability, the app text is "text that nobody
  could get mad at".
- Open: Zoom cadence (one group call a week?), who hosts (Coach Yinka /
  Gary / both), day and time (coaching-call slots are already an open
  item), whether Elite and Complete share the call.
- Note: Skool was DECIDED Sept 15 as community only, $9/mo Hobby plan,
  open access including lapsed clients. Fits.

## 3. No training setup: "all they have is a ball and a few yards"
- Problem: no at-home setup.
- What he said: create session templates for when all they have is a
  ball and a few yards. Package it under a sexier bundle name.
- Note: the bank already carries needs_wall per drill and the builder
  respects has_wall. A no-wall, no-cones, tiny-space mode is the same
  idea extended (a "space" / "equipment" setting on the player).
- Open: name; whether this is a player setting the builder honors or a
  separate shelf of sessions; whether cones (or shoes as cones) count as
  "a few yards".

## 4. See before you pay
- Problem: want to test without payment risk.
- What he said: a same-day refund promise once the training is uploaded
  (i.e. once their first week is in the app), if the parent or player
  thinks it's not a good fit.
- Note: same-day refund is already policy; this makes it a named,
  stated promise tied to a moment (first week delivered).
- Open: exact wording of the window ("same day you receive week 1"?).

## 5. Drill feedback: how do we give live training feedback?
- Problem: how can I get feedback on my drills.
- What he said, two parts: (a) they can always text one on one, a video
  of them doing the drill, and we give feedback on it by text ("mirror
  Coach Gary"); (b) a generic but very dialed-in checklist, the Strive
  standard, visible whenever they train and wonder if they're doing it
  right.
- Open: who answers the videos and the response-time promise; whether
  the checklist is per drill (cues already exist on every bank drill)
  or one universal standard; where it lives in the app.

## 6. Scared of paying monthly
- Problem: don't want to get caught up in a subscription.
- What he said: pay in full at a discount vs monthly; monthly can cancel
  anytime. "It's about low risk."
- Open: the pay-in-full term and price (3 months? 6? 12? number not
  given; Complete already has $1,500 PIF vs $600/mo, Elite has none).

## 7. Parent accountability: I don't want to police my kid
- Problem: if I have to police it, I'm the one doing the work.
- What he said: the weekly check-in text, AND a bi-weekly progress video:
  every two weeks the player films the same drill, we send that video to
  the parent so they see progress, and post it in the Skool community.
- Open: which drill (one fixed benchmark drill per player? per pillar?),
  who sends it (app or coach), consent for posting a minor's video in
  Skool, where the videos are stored.

## 8. Missed sessions and breaks
- Problem: what if I miss sessions, will my progress score be ruined.
- What he said: "how to take a vacation and pick up where you left off".
  Progress should never be messed up by a break. The progress meter
  switches from percentages to counting minutes / hours trained on
  certain things: it tracks what's worked, not what's missed.
  Accountability is handled by the Zooms and text, not by the score.
- Note: the plan already resumes after a miss (no makeup schedule). The
  progress model today is 0-100 per pillar; this is a real change to
  what progress means.
- Open: minutes vs sessions vs reps; whether the pillar ratings go away
  or sit alongside; a "pause" state for vacations.

## 9. Strength, injury prevention, speed (the big one)
- Problem: players and parents who need strength work or injury
  prevention.
- What he said: give access to our fitness plan for athletes, our injury
  prevention routine, and a speed course so they get faster. "We will
  have to build these things. I don't want to just make these things
  without having the proper videos. Everything we put into the course
  has to have true value."
- Note: the Strengthening pillar exists (empty, Oct 7). The bodyweight
  blocks are written. Speed is currently "trained by the plyo warm-ups"
  in the methodology; a speed course is new.
- Open: scope of each (how many sessions, filmed by whom), whether they
  live as bank pillars (Strengthening exists; Speed would be new), as
  on-demand programs, or as structured courses with their own progress.

## 10. Immediate gratification on day one
- Problem: right now a new member gets nothing the moment they join.
- What he said: access to the Skool community of high-level, ambitious
  players; some of his Division I players in there to start it on a
  good note; a player profile post for every new member (their photo on
  Instagram and their game highlights), introduced to the guys on
  Strive Elite. "Obviously I want ideas."
- Note: alumni names are sales-call and VSL only, never public (Sept 29
  rule). A D1 player inside a private community is a different context
  from a public ad, but it still needs their yes. The Player Highlight
  cover series already exists as the visual format.
- Open: what else lands on day one (the first week itself, a welcome
  video, the profile post), whether the profile post is Instagram
  (public) or Skool (members), consent from each family.

## 11. Film IQ
- Problem: players whose real struggle is reading the game.
- What he said: once a week, a hosted film room session breaking down
  three clips per player that they send in beforehand.
- Note: CONFLICT to resolve. Sept 30 decision: Strive Elite is the APP
  ONLY, film room and film breakdowns are Complete. This adds a weekly
  group film session to Elite. Needs an explicit re-decision on the
  Elite / Complete boundary (Complete's monthly PRIVATE breakdown can
  stay the premium version).
- Open: cadence and host, how clips are submitted (Skool, app upload),
  group size cap, the Complete boundary.

## Standing instructions for this project (his words)
- These are many different opportunities, addressed individually, with
  utmost attention and creativity. Skip nothing.
- Implement step by step, best way first, actively bringing sharper ways
  to do each one as we go.
- If something is not ready to implement, build to the point where it
  is, with no holes in the product.
- Everything added must have true value, proper videos, nothing hollow.
