# The Strive app's voice

Draft, Nov 28 2026. Built from Coach Yinka's answers to the ten moments in
`voice-quiz.md`. His to approve before the app is rewritten to it.

## In one line

A teammate who is a year ahead of you. Hypes the work, calls you out with a
question, never sounds like a brand. His own short version of the app:
"Strava for ballers" (said out loud, in DMs and captions; never in the App
Store listing, the app's name or paid ads).

## The rules

1. **Every line has a job: motivate or instigate.** A line that only
   reports is a receipt. Receipts are fine for settings, errors and
   payments, never where the app is meant to move the player.
2. **One number a line.** The number is the hook ("1h 15m today"). Two
   numbers in a row read as spam.
3. **Say the names.** The friend, the player's club, the other club.
4. **Two shapes work.**
   - The fact, then a question: "Trained 45m just now. You good with that?"
   - The hard fact, then the rally: "You're getting outworked by Haymarket
     08s. Let's go."
5. **A jab needs a real name and a real number**, and it is aimed at the
   one who is not training. To a player who just trained, the app is
   supportive first: "keep it up", "let's work".
6. **A win is personal.** When a player earns something, nobody else is in
   the line. Comparing at that moment is the cringe ("Nico's still on 3").
7. **The ask sounds like the app, not an ad.** Plain, and logical: praise
   the work, then the reason ("what you need, not what's comfortable").
8. **The app presses one notch softer than a friend would.** "Your weak
   foot won't work on itself", not "isn't fixing itself".
9. **A mix.** Every moment has at least three lines and rotates through
   them. The same line every day is the real turn-off.
10. **Leans toward how boys talk, and reads fine to a girl.** The energy
    stays. "Bro", "boys", "dawg" and "king" stay out of the app's own voice.
11. **Never a cold slogan** ("Your move."), an announcement ("Train with me
    on Strive Elite."), or a rule in place of the hook ("opens when you post
    yours today").
12. **Only what is true.** "Best week yet", "outworking you today" and
    "just passed you" are said only when the numbers say so.

## Words

**We use:** outwork, the competition, locked in (with a number beside it),
getting work in, let's go, let's work, grind, on the come up, hits
different, excellent work, run it back, in the books, just the beginning,
what you need, not what's comfortable.

**We never use:** your move, bro, boys, dawg, king, pillars,
non-negotiable, consistency, unlock, journey, crush it, beast mode, any
exclamation mark, any emoji.

**Strive Pro's selling pieces, his words (Nov 28):** "drills you can do at
home" ("dont forget that selling piece"), "what you need, not what's
comfortable", "videos to every session", "very easy to follow", full
courses for the areas a player is struggling in.

**Strive Pro:** "AI coach" may be said (his ruling, Nov 28). Coach Gary is
"a U19 Bundesliga player", and his photo may sit beside the line. "8
months" is not used until Coach Yinka says what it counts.

## Ten model lines, one per moment

Each moment shows the lines it rotates through.

1. **A friend passed you (push).** "Nico just passed you" / "Trained 45m
   just now. You good with that?"
2. **You just posted.** "45 minutes locked in. Excellent work." · "Posted.
   Keep outworking the competition." · when it is true: "45 minutes in. You
   just passed Nico."
3. **The other club is ahead.** "Haymarket 08s is up 12 minutes a player.
   Is Renegados FC letting that happen?" · "Renegados FC, you're getting
   outworked by Haymarket 08s. Let's go."
4. **The upgrade card.** "You put in the work. Make sure it's on what you
   need, not what's comfortable." then "A week built around your weak
   foot."
5. **A friend posted (push).** "Diego's getting work in" / "1h 15m today.
   You training today?" · "Diego is outworking you today" / "1h 15m. Let's
   go."
6. **The invite a player sends.** "Bet you can't outwork me this week. Get
   on Strive Elite. Code: KAI123" · "Let's grind on Strive Elite. Code:
   KAI123"
7. **No friends on the app yet.** "It hits different when the whole friend
   group is locked in. Add a teammate."
8. **Last week's card.** "Best week yet: 4h 10m. You outworked Nico and
   Diego. Run it back." · "4h 10m last week. Only Kai did more. This week
   that changes."
9. **Today's session is waiting (push).** "Your weak foot won't work on
   itself" / "Session 2 is ready. 22 minutes." · a coach's own line, then
   the same second line. A tap starts the session.
10. **A medal.** "5 hours of work nobody made you do. That's the
    difference." · "5 hours trained. It stays yours. Just the beginning."
