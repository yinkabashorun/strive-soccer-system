The problem

Team training 3-4x a week is not close to enough to achieve your goals.

Nobody coaches the hours between practices.

Team practice is built for fifteen players on one field. The weak foot and the first touch do not move there.

Training alone has no structure and no feedback.

A player guesses what to do, does it wrong, and nobody sees it.

Parents cannot tell if it is working.

Who makes him do it, and how do you know it is paying off before the season is over?

The hook is the same line as the page and the outro. The three cards are the three parent problems from the Oct 11 list: no structure between practices, no feedback when training alone, no way to see progress.
