# Gary's clip for the VSL (up to 45 seconds)

Gary to camera, phone is fine, good light, same orientation as the VSL.
About 120 words, 40 to 45 seconds at a normal pace. One take per
paragraph, cut between. He should say it his own way. The one thing that
has to land: he and Coach Yinka built this together, out of what they
both know.

Rules: no em dashes, no "AI" or "automated", no roster or scholarship
promises.

---

I'm Coach Gary. I played for Armenia's youth national teams and in the
under-19 Bundesliga. I know exactly what it took to get there. And most of
it happened alone, at night, when nobody was watching.

Nobody told me what to work on. I guessed. I wasted years guessing.

That's the part nobody coaches. The six days between practices, when a
kid either gets better or just gets older. So Coach Yinka and I put
everything we know into one place. Strive Elite.

Every Monday his week is waiting. Four sessions, every drill a video of
one of us showing him exactly how. And every week, you get a text on
what he did.

He's getting both of us. Two coaches who've been where he's trying to
go, telling him what's next.

---

## Why it's written this way

Line one earns the right to talk. "I wasted years guessing" is the
wound, and it's Gary's, so it lands without blaming anyone. "Gets better
or just gets older" is the parent's fear said plainly. Then the two of
you closing that gap, then what the player actually sees on Monday, then
the parent picturing their own son opening it.

## Notes for Gary

- "I wasted years guessing" only if it's true for him. If not, swap in
  whatever is true. Specific beats polished.
- "A video of one of us showing him exactly how" is accurate only if Gary
  is in some of the drill videos. If every video is Coach Yinka, say
  "a video showing him exactly how."
- If he has a real memory of training alone, a parking lot, a wall, a
  specific night, one sentence of that after "nobody was watching" does
  more than anything written here.
