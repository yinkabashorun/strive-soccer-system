## Caption (draft)

Every player has one thing holding them back. Say it's your weak foot.

Train a bit of everything and it gets two drills a week. Two. That is why it is the same foot it was three months ago.

Give it eight of your twelve drills for two weeks. Make week two harder than week one. Keep one drill a session for the rest of your game so nothing goes cold. Then move to the next thing.

That is how I came back from six months out with a hip tear. It is how Coach Gary and I build every Strive Elite player's three months.

DM ELITE and tell us your one thing.
