## Caption (draft)

I tore my hip and was out for six months. I had two months to get ready for a more competitive team, after a season on the bench at my old one. I came back better and ended up starting.

It was not more hours. It was the structure. One thing at a time, with the rest kept moving.

Coach Gary and I built Strive Elite on it. Three months. Two weeks on each focus. Two of every three drills go at that focus. The work steps up every second week. Strength and speed run beside it for as long as your player needs.

It works the same for a player coming back and a player in form. If your player is coming back from an injury, we start once the doctor or PT clears them to train.

DM ELITE and tell us what holds your player back most. We'll tell you where we'd start.
