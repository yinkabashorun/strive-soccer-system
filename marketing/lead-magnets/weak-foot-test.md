# The 60-Second Weak Foot Test (lead magnet draft)

Built with `.claude/skills/lead-magnet`. DRAFT for Coach Yinka to approve.
Nothing here is posted, sent or set up in GHL yet.

- **Who it is for:** the parent of a club player (about 12 to 15, on a
  competitive team) who follows @strivesoccerfc or visited /demo and has not
  booked a call.
- **Type:** reveal a problem. The test shows the gap between the strong foot
  and the weak foot in a number the parent can see.
- **Why it leads to the call:** it shows the gap and leaves the parent
  wanting to know how to close it, which is the week we build. The fix is
  never in the free piece.
- **Delivered as:** a short guide by DM (information), plus the parent's
  numbers coming back to us (which qualifies them).

## 3. The auto-reply DM (sends the test)

Hey, here's the test. It takes one minute.

1. Set up: one ball and two cones (or two shoes) about one big step apart.
2. Strong foot: 30 seconds of inside-outside touches, the ball going side to side between the cones. Count every touch.
3. Weak foot: the same 30 seconds with the weak foot only. Count every touch.
4. Send us both numbers.

Quick 3 questions so we can read it right:
- How old is your player?
- What team and level do they play on?
- What are they training for (next team, college, academy)?

---

## 4. The follow-up DM (after the numbers come back)

**Qualified** (club player and a real goal):

Thanks for running it. [Strong] on the strong foot and [Weak] on the weak foot, so that's the gap we'd go after first. That gap is exactly what we build a week around, outside team training. Want to jump on a quick call so we can show you the plan to close it?

**Not a fit yet** (younger, or no team or goal yet):

Thanks for running it. Keep doing the test once a week and send us the numbers, you'll see it move. When they're ready for a full week built around them, we're here.

**No numbers after 2 days:**

Did your player get a chance to try the test? One minute, both feet. Send the two numbers and we'll tell you what they mean.
