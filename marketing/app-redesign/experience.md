# The Strive Elite experience (draft for Coach Yinka, Nov 21 2026)

The aim, his words: it should feel like being inside something special and exclusive, yet never hard to get into. The presentation is what makes it feel special. Using it should stay simple and feel rewarding.

How the app does that: **exclusive in the packaging, effortless in the use.** Every screen has one obvious thing to do. Everything around that one thing (the type, the gold, the motion, the timing) says this is not an ordinary app.

Motion prototype: `mock/proto.html` (open it in a browser and tap through; add `?play=1` to watch it play itself). Video of the walkthrough: `mock/proto-walkthrough.mp4` (35 s). Still screens: `mock/mock4.html`.

## 1. The structure: five moments, then one loop

1. **The door (the moment after they pay).** Black screen. The Strive mark fades in, a gold line draws, "You're in." Then their **member card** rises with a light sweeping across it: their name, "Member since Nov 2026", Pro. This is the moment it feels exclusive, and it costs them one tap: Build my week.
2. **The quiz.** One question per screen, big photo tiles. Tapping a tile is the answer: a check pops and the next question comes up by itself. There is no Next button and no form.
3. **Building.** Three lines tick off: reading your answers, picking drills for *their* weak spot, setting four sessions. It names their own weak spot, so they can see the week is built for them.
4. **The drop.** "Your week is here." The four session cards land one after another, built around their weak foot. This happens every Monday: each new week arrives as an unveiling, the way a release drops, not a list that changed quietly overnight.
5. **The loop, every session after that:**
   - **Home** shows one card and one button.
   - **Start session:** the card grows to fill the screen. "Grab your ball." A 3, 2, 1 count-in, with a tap felt on each number.
   - **One drill at a time:** the video, the sets, one big Done. Done turns into a check, the bar at the top fills gold and the next drill slides in.
   - **The finish:** the gold ring closes, the minutes count up, sparks fly, "Session done, Marcus", then the next medal and how close it is.
   - **Back home:** that session's circle fills and the time trained ticks up in front of them.

**The member card stays on the You tab** as the one constant reminder of where they belong.

## 2. What makes it feel exclusive (without gatekeeping)

- **The member card:** their name, member since, the tier. It is earned by joining, not by passing a test.
- **The Monday drop:** a cadence to look forward to, not a feed that never ends.
- **Quiet:** no ads, no upsell banners, no red badges, no streaks to guilt them, no counts of what they missed. A calm app reads as expensive; a loud one reads as cheap.
- **Real footage of Strive players:** never stock photos.
- **Gold is earned:** gold appears only on what the player has done (rings, ticks, medals, the bar of the current week), never on a button. When gold shows up, it means something.
- **Their name and their weak spot in the words:** "Session done, Marcus", "built around your weak foot".

## 3. What keeps it easy

- One main button per screen, always in the same place, in reach of the thumb.
- Nothing to set up: the quiz is a handful of taps, and after that the app always knows what is next.
- Every screen opens on what they need right now: today's session, not a menu.
- Back always works, and closing a session keeps their place.

## 4. The motion rules (for the build)

- **One feel everywhere:** a soft ease-out for things that move into place, and a small spring for things that pop (checks, medals, the circle filling). Nothing bounces more than once.
- **Speed:** a tap answers within 0.15 s; a screen change takes 0.3 to 0.5 s; only the rewards take longer (the ring closing about 1.1 s, the card rising about 0.9 s), because those are the moments to enjoy.
- **Movement that explains:** a screen pushed in comes from the right and goes back to the right; the session grows out of its card; Done carries the drill off to the left. Each move tells them where they are.
- **Tabs switch instantly:** a short crossfade, never a page load or a spinner.
- **Loading:** grey shapes of the screen, never a spinner; drill posters show at once, the next drill loads ahead.
- **Taps you can feel (the App Store app and Android):**
  - a light tap on choices;
  - a firm tap on Done;
  - a double tap on finishing a session;
  - a heavy one on a medal;
  - one on each number of the count-in.
- **Sound:** off, except the set timer's tones, which they can turn off.
- **Reduced motion:** when the phone asks for less motion, every move becomes a plain fade.

## 5. What is new against the app today, to build

| Piece | Today | In this design |
|---|---|---|
| After paying | straight to the intake form | the door: "You're in" plus the member card |
| Quiz | a form with fields | one question per screen, tap to answer |
| First week | a "being built" line | the building screen, then the drop |
| Monday | the week changes quietly | the drop screen, once per new week |
| Session start | opens the player | the card grows into it, then the count-in |
| Done | ticks and moves on | the check, the gold bar, the next drill slides in |
| Finish | ring and minutes | the same, plus sparks, the medal rising and a felt tap |
| You | progress | the member card at the top |

## Open, his call

- **"Member since"** is a real date. A member number on the card was left off on purpose: it would be a number we have to keep true.
- **The 3, 2, 1 count-in:** can it be skipped with a tap? Proposed yes.
- **The drop:** shown once per new week, then Home. Proposed yes.
- The names and numbers in the mock are made up.
