# Who leads each number, and what we copy (Nov 24 2026)

Coach Yinka: "Be creative and copy the leaders in each of the metrics we are
implementing and do so to the highest standard you possibly can."

A research pass (web, read-only). Confidence: [P] primary source read,
[S] reputable secondary or a search excerpt of the primary, [U] could not
be verified. Five hosts were blocked from the session (research.duolingo.com,
blog.duolingo.com, lennysnewsletter.com, reachcapital.com,
threadreaderapp.com), so their content came through excerpts only.

House rules that decide what we may copy: no streaks, no points, nothing
that counts what was missed, no guilt, no made-up urgency or numbers, paying
members ranked against nobody, notifications inform and never order or sell,
one upgrade prompt in the free app, the players are minors.

## 1. Activation: time to the first session
- Duolingo: a lesson before an account; moving sign-up back was worth about
  +20% daily users. [S]
- Calm: a "set a daily reminder" step right after the first session. 40% set
  one and retained about three times better, still holding twelve weeks out
  (the prompt test is the evidence; the first correlation was not causal). [S]
- Peloton: badges at 1, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100 classes; "golden behaviours" in
  the first weeks. [S]

WE COPY: "When do you usually train?" on the first finish screen, which sets
the reminder hour from day one; the First Session medal; the sessions
ladder 25, 50, 100; the drop's one button starts session 1.

## 2. Finishing a session that was started
- No company publishes this. The research: goal gradient and endowed
  progress (a card that starts part-filled is finished faster). [P]
- Nike Training Club: length, focus and equipment before the start. [S]

WE COPY: the session card says its real drills and minutes before Start;
a half-done session resumes at the next drill; the daily reminder says
"Session 2 is where you left it. 2 drills to go."

## 3. Finishing the week
- Apple Fitness: a personal goal, never a rank; rings can be paused for up
  to 90 days without losing awards (our break rule). [S]
- Peloton: members using two or more disciplines a month churn about 60%
  less. [S]

WE COPY: "One more session completes your week." stays the only weekly
nudge; after a full week the app points at Extra work and a course
(breadth). DROPPED: streak awards, "you can still do it" coaching lines.

## 4. Weeks trained (the habit)
- Duolingo: "protect the channel": wording and timing are tested freely,
  volume is not raised without the top sign-off. Reminders stop when a
  person is quiet for a week. Fresh wording wins, so lines rotate. [S]

WE COPY: the reminder at the player's own hour; reminders pause themselves
after a quiet stretch with one plain last line; the channel rule is written
into docs/product-principles.md. DROPPED: streaks, streak savers, leagues.

## 5. Proof of progress
- Strava Matched Activities: repeat efforts on the same route are grouped
  on their own and the trend shown. [P]
- WHOOP: a weekly assessment every Monday. [S]

WE COPY: the Monday drop says what last week was ("Last week you finished 3
sessions and trained 1h 4m."). NOT BUILT, his call: the side-by-side of the
same drill built without being asked, on the finish screen and for the
parent (the progress clip lives on the website and on Elite only today).

## 6. Still a member at weeks 4, 8 and 12
- Peloton: engagement is the stated churn predictor. [S]
- Future: reviewers name the human coach's messages as why they stay
  (reviews only). [U]

NOT BUILT, his call: one human note from a coach at day 3 and at week 3
(Strive Pro has no coach chat by his decision), and proof sent to the
parent before each renewal.

## 7. The free app: staying, bringing friends, the parent paying
- Strava: new users who gave kudos, trained with others or joined a club in
  their first weeks were about twice as likely to be active six months
  later. [S]
- BeReal: friends hidden until you post. [S]
- Cash App and Dropbox: the reward counts only once the new person has
  really used the product. [P/S]
- Greenlight and Apple Ask to Buy: the teen starts, the parent finishes and
  pays; the request can be sent again. [S/P]

WE COPY: a first-days list in the free app (post, add friends, join a club,
give props); the squad month counts a teammate only after a first training
post; Send to a parent, re-sendable. DROPPED: BeReal's random two-minute
window and the "late" label.

## 8. Notifications: saying yes and not turning them off
- Apple: ask in context, never at launch; "avoid sending multiple
  notifications for the same thing"; "avoid sending a notification that
  tells people to perform specific tasks"; Time Sensitive only for
  something happening now. [P]
- Duolingo: its best push is the one that says the reminders will stop. [S]

WE COPY: every notice checked against Apple's two sentences; one notice per
event enforced by the database; quiet delivery at night; reminders that
pause themselves; choices per topic in Account.
