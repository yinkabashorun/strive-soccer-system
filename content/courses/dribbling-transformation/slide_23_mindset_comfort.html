Comfort
Zone

When a drill feels awkward, that is not a sign you are doing it wrong. That is a sign you are doing it right. Discomfort means your brain is building something new.

Most players stay comfortable. They practice what they are already good at. That is why most players plateau. Growth only happens at the edge of what you can currently do.

"If it feels easy,
you're not growing."

Lean into the drills that feel hardest. The Elastico, the Maradona, the Reverse Elastico — these are the moves that will separate you. Stay with them longer than feels comfortable.
