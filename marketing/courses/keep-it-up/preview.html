The Juggling Ladder

Juggling is the fastest way to a better first touch. Every level on this ladder is one small thing done cleanly, filmed so you can see exactly what passing looks like. Eight levels, easiest to hardest. Every level has a pass mark. You hit the mark, you move up.

How it works
  1. Start at Level 1, even if you can already juggle. The early levels are where the clean contact gets built.
  2. Most levels have a Beginner drill and an Advanced drill. Pass the Beginner, then the Advanced, then move up.
  3. A pass mark is in a row, with no drops. If you drop it, that attempt is over and the count starts again.
  4. Five minutes a day is enough. Juggling pays you back for every day, not for one long day.
  5. Film the day you pass a level. In two months you will want to see where you started.

One touch

working name

Normal one-touch juggling, for players learning it. Beginner only.

Beginner

Drop, touch, catch

Ball in your hands. Drop it, one touch, catch it at your chest. Repeat.

Cue: toe up, ankle locked, hit the middle of the ball.

Pass: 10 in a row, caught at your chest without reaching.

Two touches each foot

working name

Two touches on each foot, with a catch first, then without stopping.

Beginner

Two touches each foot, catch

Drop it, two touches, catch. Repeat, working both feet.

Cue: the first touch is small, the second one sets up the catch.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Clip 2, first half

Advanced

Two touches each foot, keep it up

Same pattern with no catch. Two on the right, two on the left, keep it going.

Cue: small touches, stay on the balls of your feet.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

Weak foot

working name

The same two-touch pattern on the weaker foot, with a catch first, then without stopping.

Beginner

Weak foot, two touches, catch

Drop it, two touches with your weaker foot, catch. Repeat.

Cue: slow down, the weak foot needs a second longer.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Low and tight, keep it up

Keep it up with the ball never above your knee.

Cue: quick feet, quiet touches, do not let it climb.

Height

working name

One touch sent high and caught, then juggling at height without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, high touch, catch

Drop it, one touch sent above your head, catch it on the way down. Repeat.

Cue: same contact, more leg, eyes on the ball the whole way.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Clip 9

Advanced

Waist height, keep it up

Keep it up with every touch reaching your waist, both feet.

Cue: same contact every time, let it drop, do not chase it.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

5

Thigh

working name

The thigh as a surface: one thigh and a catch, then thighs sent high without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, thigh, catch

Drop it onto your thigh, let it pop up, catch. Repeat.

Cue: knee up, thigh flat like a table, ball lands on the middle.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Clip 7, first half

Advanced

Thighs high, keep it up

Keep it up on your thighs with every touch sent to head height.

Cue: drive the knee, catch it on the flat of the thigh, wait for it to come down.

Pass: 15 without a drop.

Clip 7, second half

6

Thigh control

working name

Two thigh touches with a catch, then thighs only without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, foot, thigh, thigh, catch

Drop it, foot, thigh, thigh, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the first thigh has to be clean or the second one never comes.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Clip 5

Advanced

Thighs only, keep it up

Keep it up on your thighs only, both legs, no feet.

Cue: stay tall, bring the knee to the ball, do not lean back.

Pass: 20 thigh touches without a drop.

7

Feet and thighs

working name

Foot to thigh with a catch, then feet and thighs together without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, foot, thigh, catch

Drop it, one foot touch up to your thigh, thigh, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the foot touch is soft, it only needs to reach the thigh.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Clip 3

Advanced

Feet and thighs, keep it up

Keep it up with feet and thighs, no catch. The foot sets it up, the thigh keeps it going, back to the foot.

Cue: let the height tell you the surface, low is feet, high is thighs.

Pass: 30 touches without a drop.

Clip 4, whole take

8

Head

working name

Advanced only. The hardest surface.

Advanced

Head it up

Keep it up with your forehead.

Cue: eyes open, forehead not the top of the head, knees soft.

Pass: 10 headers without a drop.
