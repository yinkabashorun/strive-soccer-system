# The copy quiz: finding the app's voice

Coach Yinka, Nov 27 2026: "id like to diffrientiate the app by speaking in
copy our user resonates with. and im the perfect person to quiz for that
since i know the consumer in and out because i was just the consumer last
year." Nothing too different from what the app says today.

## How it runs

- Ten real moments in the app, one at a time.
- Each moment is written four ways, each a small step in a different
  direction: plain, a teammate talking, cold and short, the fact with a
  number.
- Numbering: moment N, versions NA to ND, and three questions every time:
  - Q1. Which one makes you open the app?
  - Q2. Which one makes you turn notifications off (or cringe)?
  - Q3. How would you actually have texted it to a teammate?
- At the end: a one-page voice guide (the rules, the words we use, the
  words we never use, ten model lines), added to the COPY RULE with a test,
  then the app is rewritten to it.

## Moment 1: a friend just passed you on the board (a push)

- 1A. "Nico just passed you. One session takes it back."
- 1B. "Nico just went past you. You good with that?"
- 1C. "Nico passed you. Your move."
- 1D. "Nico's ahead of you now. He trained 45 minutes today."

His answers:
- Q1 (opens the app): 1B and 1D.
- Q2 (turns notifications off): 1C.
- Q3 (his own words): "bro fell off his training, bros not locked, theres a
  new king to the gc"

What it says about the voice (my reading, to be checked against the next
moments):
- A teammate's jab, asked as a question, works. So does a plain fact with
  a number in it.
- A cold slogan ("Your move.") is the turn-off: it sounds like a brand, not
  a friend.
- His own words are group chat talk: "locked", "fell off", "king of the gc".
  Status among friends is the prize.

Used so far: the push itself now reads "Nico just passed you" / "Trained
45m just now. You good with that?" (the fact, then the jab).
