Keep It Up

The Juggling Ladder

Juggling is the fastest way to a better first touch. Every level on this ladder is one pattern done cleanly, filmed so you can see exactly what passing looks like. Seven levels, easiest to hardest. Every drill has a pass mark. You hit the mark, you move up.

How it works
  1. Start at Level 1, even if you can already juggle. The early levels are where the clean contact gets built.
  2. Every pattern comes twice. First with a catch after each rep, then with no catch, keeping it up. Pass the catch version, then the keep it up version, then move up.
  3. A pass mark is in a row, with no drops. If you drop it, that attempt is over and the count starts again.
  4. Five minutes a day is enough. Juggling pays you back for every day, not for one long day.
  5. Film the day you pass a level. In two months you will want to see where you started.
1

One touch

Normal one-touch juggling, for players learning it.

Beginner

One touch, catch

Ball in your hands. Drop it, one touch, catch it at your chest. Repeat.

Cue: toe up, ankle locked, hit the middle of the ball.

Pass: 10 in a row, caught at your chest without reaching.

2

Two touches each foot

Two touches on one foot, then two on the other.

Beginner

Two touches each foot, catch

Drop it, two touches on one foot, catch. Repeat on the other foot.

Cue: the first touch is small, the second one sets up the catch. Same contact on both feet.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Two touches each foot, keep it up

Same pattern with no catch. Two on the right, two on the left, keep it going.

Cue: small touches, stay on the balls of your feet.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

3

One, two

One touch with your right foot, two touches with your weak foot.

Beginner

One, two, catch

Drop it, one touch with your right foot, two touches with your weak foot, catch. Repeat.

Cue: right, left, left. Count it out loud until it is automatic.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

One, two, keep it up

Same pattern with no catch. One touch right foot, two touches weak foot, keep it going.

Cue: right, left, left, over and over. Slow down before the weak foot.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

4

Laces, inside

One touch with the laces, one with the inside of the foot.

Beginner

Laces, inside, catch

Drop it, one touch with the laces, one touch with the inside of the foot, catch. Repeat.

Cue: laces send it up, the inside brings it back. Ankle locked on both.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Laces, inside, keep it up

Same pattern with no catch. Laces, inside, laces, inside, keep it going.

Cue: turn the foot for the inside touch, then straight back to the laces.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

5

Thigh

The thigh as a surface. One thigh and a catch, then thighs sent high without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, thigh, catch

Drop it onto your thigh, let it pop up, catch. Repeat.

Cue: knee up, thigh flat like a table, ball lands on the middle.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Thighs high, keep it up

Keep it up on your thighs with every touch sent to head height.

Cue: drive the knee, catch it on the flat of the thigh, wait for it to come down.

Pass: 15 without a drop.

6

Thighs, laces

Thigh then laces. Three steps: catch, catch, then keep it up.

Beginner

Thighs, laces, catch

Drop it onto your thigh, then one touch with the laces, catch. Repeat.

Cue: thigh flat like a table, then meet the ball with the laces on the way down.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Intermediate

Laces, thigh, catch

Drop it, one touch with the laces up to your thigh, thigh, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the laces touch is soft, it only needs to reach the thigh.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Thighs, laces, keep it up

Same pattern with no catch. Thigh, laces, thigh, laces, keep it going.

Cue: the thigh sets the height, the laces keep the rhythm. Stay tall.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

7

Head

Advanced only. The hardest surface.

Advanced

Head it up

Keep it up with your forehead.

Cue: eyes open, forehead not the top of the head, knees soft.

Pass: 10 headers without a drop.
