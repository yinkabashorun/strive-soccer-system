The problem

Team training 3-4x a week is not close to enough to achieve your goals.

Nobody coaches the hours between practices.

Practice is built for fifteen players. The weak foot and the first touch do not move there.

Training alone has no structure and no feedback.

A player guesses what to do, does it wrong, and nobody sees it.

Parents cannot tell if it is working.

Who makes him do it, and how do you know it is paying off?
