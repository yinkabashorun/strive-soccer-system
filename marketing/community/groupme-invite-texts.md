# GroupMe invite texts (Oct 16 2026)

One GroupMe group named "Strive Elite", players and parents together.
What it is for, Coach Yinka's words: player wins, motivation, and
events. Nothing else. Clips and questions stay in the app chat with the
coach. Coach Yinka adds each person by phone number; no app needed,
messages arrive as texts. Admin only adds, share link off. Under-13s
are in through the parent's number.

## Parent (sent before the add)

Hey [Parent], I'm adding you and [First] to the Strive Elite GroupMe
today. It's where the wins get posted, where I keep the group fired
up, and where you'll hear about events first. Nothing to download, you
get a text from GroupMe to join. Let's keep [First] dialed in.

## Player with his own phone

[First], I'm adding you to the Strive Elite GroupMe today. Wins go up
there, and that's where you'll hear about events first. You'll get a
text from GroupMe to join. Let's get yours up there.

## Old and lapsed clients (parent)

Hey [Parent], it's Coach Yinka. The Strive players and families have a
GroupMe now for wins and events, and I'd like [First] back around the
group. It's free. Reply yes and I'll add you both today. Good to have
[First] back with us.

## Rules carried
- "Your player" never "your child". First names only. No sign-off,
  forward-looking close. No emojis, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
- Alumni and D1 names are never in an invite. A D1 alum joins only on
  his own yes.
- Nobody is added without a text first, so the GroupMe invite never
  lands cold.
