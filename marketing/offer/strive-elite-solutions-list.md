# Strive Elite: Solutions list (Step 3 of the offer build)

Coach Yinka's own notebook, Oct 11 2026:

- Dream outcome: become the most dominant player on your team in 12 weeks.
- Decreased time: 12 weeks dominant, 6 weeks confident.
- Eight problems, in his words (parent and player mixed, as they come).

The book's move: reverse every problem into "How to ..." solution language.
Nothing is built yet at this step. This is the list of things we will have
to show someone how to do, and the list of things Strive Elite has to
actually solve. Step 4 is deciding the delivery vehicle for each one.

## PROBLEM TO SOLUTION

### 1. I already have team training. I don't want to overwork myself.
- ... is too much on top of club: How to add the work that actually moves
  you without adding hours (four focused sessions a week, 40 minutes,
  built around what you need, not more of what you already do)
- ... will burn him out: How to train at home without ever running on
  empty for practice or games (the plan knows your team schedule and sits
  around it)
- ... team training should be enough: How to get what team training can't
  give you, the individual work on your specific gap, with a full team
  on one field
- ... I'll overdo it and get hurt: How to make the extra work protect
  your body, not wear it down (warm-up first every session, a
  strengthening finisher, never jumping or sprinting through an injury)

### 2. I'm already training on my own with structure.
- ... I have a routine: How to stop guessing whether your routine is the
  right routine (a week built around your actual weaknesses, by two
  coaches who have watched thousands of players)
- ... I know what to work on: How to know what to work on NEXT, every
  Monday, instead of repeating what you already do well
- ... mine is free: How to turn solo reps into measurable progress you
  and your parents can see (progress by pillar, week over week)
- ... I don't want to be told what to do: How to keep your own
  discipline and add direction to it (your reps, our plan)

### 3. I won't be able to hold my kid accountable to the training.
- ... I'm not there when he trains: How to know exactly what got done
  this week without standing over him (a text to you every week, what
  he finished, what he missed)
- ... he won't listen to me: How to make the coach the voice, not the
  parent (the plan and the check-ins come from us, you just read the
  text)
- ... I'll forget to check: How to never have to check (it comes to you)
- ... accountability feels like nagging: How to make accountability feel
  like progress, not punishment (a full week is celebrated, a missed one
  gets "let's get him back on track", never shame)

### 4. I don't have the at-home setup.
- ... I don't have space: How to run every session in a driveway, a
  patch of grass, or a garage (nothing needs more than a few yards)
- ... I don't have equipment: How to do the whole program with a ball
  and a few cones (shoes work as cones; a wall is a bonus, never a
  requirement)
- ... I don't have a wall: How to get the full week without a wall (the
  plan knows, and never assigns wall work you can't do)
- ... I'd have to buy things to find out: How to know before you pay
  that your space works (we ask at signup, the first week fits what you
  told us)

### 5. How can I get feedback on my drills?
- ... nobody sees him do it: How to do every drill right without a coach
  in the yard (a video of the exact drill, cues on the card, three
  drills a session so each one gets real attention)
- ... I can't tell if his form is right: How to check your own form
  against the demo, every rep (watch, do, watch again)
- ... I want a coach to look: How to get a coach's eyes on your reps when
  you need them (message us in the app; on Complete, a monthly private
  film breakdown)
- ... feedback takes forever: How to get a coach's answer the same day,
  not next season

### 6. I don't want to get caught up in a subscription.
- ... I'll be locked in: How to train month to month with no contract
  (cancel anytime, no questions)
- ... I'll forget and keep paying: How to never pay for a month you don't
  want (cancel in a text to us, done the same day)
- ... what if it's not for us: How to try it with zero risk (same-day
  refund if it's not for you)
- ... the price will creep: How to lock your rate for life ($249 a month
  if you join by Nov 1, never raised on you)

### 7. If I have to police it, I'm the one doing the work.
- ... the program should do the pushing: How to make the plan push the
  player, not the parent (a new week every Monday, the next session
  always waiting, reminders from us)
- ... I'm already the ride and the fee payer: How to add nothing to your
  plate except reading one text a week
- ... he needs someone watching: How to make him feel watched without
  you watching (every session he completes is seen by his coach)
- ... it will become a fight at home: How to keep the fight out of your
  house (the coach sets the expectation, the parent stays the parent)

### 8. What if I miss some sessions? Will my progress score be ruined?
- ... I'll fall behind: How to miss a week and pick up exactly where you
  left off (no makeup schedule, no catch-up guilt)
- ... the score will punish me: How to make progress count what you
  built, not what you skipped (a missed session is a missed session, it
  is not subtracted from what you can already do)
- ... tournament weekends and travel: How to keep training through a
  heavy week (the plan flexes, the drills travel, a ball and a few
  yards anywhere)
- ... I'll feel like a failure and quit: How to come back after a bad
  week and have it mean nothing (the next Monday is a new week, every
  time)

## Two things to notice before step 4

1. Almost every solution above already exists in the product. That's
   the point of the exercise: the offer isn't missing features, the
   copy is missing the parent's words. Step 4 is choosing how each
   solution shows up (on the page, in the app, on the call) and naming
   it.
2. Three solutions are promises the product does NOT keep yet, so they
   are not safe to write on the page until they do: "the plan knows
   your team schedule" (it doesn't, it builds four sessions and the
   family picks the days), "a coach's answer the same day" (true only
   if Coach Yinka commits to it), and "the plan flexes for a tournament
   weekend" (it picks back up, it does not reschedule). Keep those as
   call language or build them.

## The dream outcome line, flagged

"Most dominant player on your team in 12 weeks" is a strong hook and the
right shape for the exercise. On the page or in an ad it is a guarantee
nobody can control (the team, the coach, the other kids), and the Oct 5
rule for Complete was "a clear path and a player ready for the moment,
never a roster or scholarship". Safe public versions of the same idea:
"the most prepared player on the field", "noticeably better in 6 weeks,
a different player in 12", "the player the coach can't leave out". Use
"dominant" on the call, where you can say what you mean.
