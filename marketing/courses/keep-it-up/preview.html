Keep It Up

The Juggling Ladder

Juggling is the fastest way to a better first touch. Every level on this ladder is one touch pattern done cleanly, filmed so you can see exactly what passing looks like. Seven levels, easiest to hardest. Every drill has a pass mark. You hit the mark, you move up.

How it works
  1. Start at Level 1, even if you can already juggle. The early levels are where the clean contact gets built.
  2. Every pattern comes twice. First with a catch at your chest after each rep, then with no catch, keeping it up. Pass the catch version, then the keep it up version, then move up.
  3. Strong foot and weak foot, not right and left. Whichever foot you trust is your strong foot.
  4. A pass mark is in a row, with no drops. If you drop it, that attempt is over and the count starts again.
  5. Five minutes a day is enough. Juggling pays you back for every day, not for one long day.
  6. Film the day you pass a level. In two months you will want to see where you started.
1

One touch

Normal one-touch juggling, for players learning it.

Beginner

One touch, catch

TouchthenCatch

Ball in your hands. Drop it, one touch, catch it at your chest. Repeat.

Cue: toe up, ankle locked, hit the middle of the ball.

Pass: 10 in a row, caught at your chest without reaching.

2

Two touches each foot

Two touches on the strong foot, then two on the weak foot.

Beginner

Two touches each foot, catch

StrongthenStrongthenCatch
WeakthenWeakthenCatch

Drop it, two touches with your strong foot, catch at your chest. Then two touches with your weak foot, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the first touch is small, the second one sends it to your chest for the catch. Same contact on both feet.

Pass: 10 in a row on each foot.

Advanced

Two touches each foot, keep it up

StrongthenStrongthenWeakthenWeakthenRepeat

No catch. Two touches strong foot, two touches weak foot, keep it going.

Cue: small touches, stay on the balls of your feet.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

3

One, two

One touch with the strong foot, two touches with the weak foot.

Beginner

One, two, catch

StrongthenWeakthenWeakthenCatch

Drop it, one touch with your strong foot, two touches with your weak foot, catch at your chest. Repeat.

Cue: say it as you go, strong, weak, weak, catch. The weak foot gets two in a row, so give its first touch a little more time.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

One, two, keep it up

StrongthenWeakthenWeakthenRepeat

No catch. One touch strong foot, two touches weak foot, keep it going.

Cue: keep the count going out loud until the weak foot stops thinking.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

4

Laces, inside

One touch with the laces, then the inside of the foot brings it to your chest.

Beginner

Laces, inside, catch

LacesthenInsidethenCatch

Drop it, one touch with the laces, then one touch with the inside of the foot that brings it up to your chest, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the laces pop it up. The inside is the touch that sends it to chest height, so the catch is easy.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Laces, inside, keep it up

LacesthenInsidethenRepeat

No catch. Laces, inside, laces, inside, keep it going.

Cue: turn the foot for the inside touch, then straight back to the laces.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

5

Thigh

The thigh as a surface. One thigh and a catch, then thighs sent high without stopping.

Beginner

Drop, thigh, catch

ThighthenCatch

Drop it onto your thigh, let it pop up, catch. Repeat.

Cue: knee up, thigh flat like a table, ball lands on the middle.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Thighs high, keep it up

Thigh, highthenThigh, highthenRepeat

Keep it up on your thighs with every touch sent to head height.

Cue: drive the knee, catch it on the flat of the thigh, wait for it to come down.

Pass: 15 without a drop.

6

Thighs, laces

Thigh and laces together. Three steps: catch, catch, then keep it up.

Beginner

Thighs, laces, catch

ThighthenLacesthenCatch

Drop it onto your thigh, then one touch with the laces, catch at your chest. Repeat.

Cue: thigh flat like a table, then meet the ball with the laces on the way down.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Intermediate

Laces, thigh, catch

Laces, to chestthenThighthenCatch

Drop it, a firm laces touch that gets the ball up to chest level, then your thigh controls it, catch. Repeat.

Cue: the laces touch is not soft, it has to reach your chest. The thigh touch is the softer one, it takes the pace off so you can catch.

Pass: 10 in a row.

Advanced

Thighs, laces, keep it up

ThighthenLacesthenRepeat

No catch. Thigh, laces, thigh, laces, keep it going.

Cue: the thigh sets the height, the laces keep the rhythm. Stay tall.

Pass: 20 touches without a drop.

7

Head

Advanced only.

Advanced

Head it up

HeadthenHeadthenRepeat

Keep it up with your forehead.

Cue: eyes open, forehead not the top of the head, knees soft.

Pass: 10 headers without a drop.
