CONTEXT: what a Strive Elite drill is. Strive Elite is at-home training
for youth soccer players, mostly 10 to 17, built by Coach Yinka and
Coach Gary. A player trains alone, in a yard, a driveway, a park, a
garage, or a room. Every drill is fully solo and needs at most a ball, a
few cones (shoes work) and sometimes a wall. A session is a plyo
warm-up plus three skill drills of about 8 to 10 minutes each, plus a
strengthening finisher when a player is managing an injury. Drills are
filmed once and shown in the app with reps and cues, so every drill must
be explainable in two sentences and demonstrable in under a minute.
The pillars and what each one is for:
- Ball Mastery: touches, control, both feet, close dribbling, cone work.
- Weak Foot: the same work, non-dominant foot only.
- Passing: weight, accuracy, first touch off a return; needs a wall
  unless it is a self-serve drill (pass, follow, collect).
- Scanning: shoulder checks before the touch, receiving on the half turn,
  knowing where space is before the ball arrives.
- Decision Making: pick late, commit fully; gates, exits, calls, options.
- Confidence: 1v1 moves at a cone at speed (feints, chops, elasticos).
- Speed: first step, change of direction, reaction, with and without
  the ball, short distances.
- Plyo: room warm-ups, jumps and hops, 4 to 5 minutes, already covered.
- Strengthening: bodyweight core, hips, glutes, groin; finishers.
A good Strive drill has one clear focus, a rep scheme a kid can follow
alone, cues a parent could read out, and gets harder by speed, by weak
foot, or by adding a scan or a decision, never by adding equipment.

=== STRENGTHENING (session finishers, bodyweight only, no ball) ===
Every title starts with "Strength: ". needs_wall false. minutes 5.
1. Strength: Dead bug | On your back, arms up, knees at 90. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly, back flat on the floor, return, switch. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Ribs down, back glued to the floor, slow on the way out
2. Strength: Front plank | Forearms and toes, straight line from head to heels. Hold. | reps: 3 x 30 sec | cues: Squeeze glutes, don't let the hips sag
3. Strength: Bird dog | On hands and knees. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back, hold two seconds, return, switch. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Hips level, no wobble, reach long
4. Strength: Hollow hold | On your back, arms overhead, legs straight and lifted, lower back pressed into the floor. Hold. | reps: 3 x 20 sec | cues: Lower back flat, chin tucked, breathe
5. Strength: Side plank | Forearm and feet stacked, hips lifted in a straight line. Hold, then switch sides. | reps: 3 x 20 sec each side | cues: Hips high, top shoulder stacked over the bottom one
6. Strength: Hip dips | From side plank, lower the hip to tap the floor and lift back up. | reps: 3 x 10 each side | cues: Controlled down, drive up through the hip
7. Strength: Shoulder taps | High plank. Tap the opposite shoulder with one hand without the hips turning. | reps: 3 x 10 each side | cues: Feet wide, hips still, slow taps
8. Strength: Bear hold | Hands and toes, knees one inch off the floor, back flat. Hold. | reps: 3 x 20 sec | cues: Knees hover, back flat like a table
9. Strength: Glute bridge | On your back, knees bent, feet flat. Drive hips up, squeeze at the top, lower slowly. | reps: 3 x 12 | cues: Squeeze at the top, ribs down, heels pushing the floor
10. Strength: Single leg bridge | Same as the bridge with one foot lifted, other knee pulled toward the chest. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Hips level, no twisting
11. Strength: Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch | One knee down, other foot forward. Tuck the hips under and shift forward until you feel the front of the back hip. Hold. | reps: 2 x 30 sec each side | cues: Tuck the tailbone, stay tall, don't arch
12. Strength: Hip flexor march | Standing tall against nothing, drive one knee to hip height and hold two seconds, lower slowly, switch. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Stay tall, don't lean back, slow down
13. Strength: 90/90 | Seated, front leg at 90 and back leg at 90. Rotate the knees side to side without using your hands, then hold each side. | reps: 2 x 8 each side plus 20 sec hold | cues: Chest tall, move from the hips not the back
14. Strength: Reverse lunge knee drive | Step back into a lunge, then drive that knee up to the chest as you stand. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Front knee over the foot, stick the balance at the top
15. Strength: Single leg RDL | Stand on one leg, hinge at the hip reaching the other leg back, flat back, return. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Flat back, hips square, soft knee
16. Strength: Lateral lunge | Step wide to one side, sit into that hip, other leg straight, push back to standing. | reps: 3 x 8 each side | cues: Chest up, knee tracks over the toes
17. Strength: Side lying leg raises | On your side, bottom knee bent, top leg straight. Lift the top leg slowly, toes forward, lower. | reps: 3 x 12 each side | cues: Toes forward, lift from the hip, slow down

=== SCANNING === needs_wall false, minutes 8
22. Scan and touch | Ball at your feet, open space. Shoulder-check before every single touch in a dribble pattern. | reps: 5 x 45 sec on, 45 sec off | cues: Check both shoulders, say what you saw out loud
23. Check and turn | Box of 4 cones, 5 yards apart. Dribble to the middle, shoulder-check both ways, exit through a different side every rep. | reps: 4 x 10 turns, rest 30 sec | cues: Check both shoulders, decide from what you saw, never the same exit twice
24. Half-turn receives | Roll the ball out in front of you. Receive on the half-turn away from imagined pressure, first touch out of your feet into space. | reps: 3 x 15 | cues: Shoulder-check before the ball arrives, open your hips, touch into space

=== DECISION MAKING === needs_wall false, minutes 10
25. Two-gate finish | Two gates of cones 5 yards apart. Attack the middle, pick one gate late, explode through it. | reps: 5 x 8, walk-back reset | cues: Decide late, commit fully, burst through the gate
26. 1v1 shadow | One cone as the defender. Dribble at it, commit it with a move, then decide: exit left, exit right, or stop and shield. | reps: 4 x 10, rest 40 sec | cues: Sell the move, decide on the way in, never the same exit twice
27. Cone-call turns | Three numbered cones around you. Dribble in place, call a number out loud (or have someone call it), turn and attack that cone. | reps: 4 x 10, rest 30 sec | cues: React on the call, first touch toward the cone, head up

=== SPEED === needs_wall false, minutes 8
28. Quick feet | Any line on the ground. Fast feet over and back, minimal ground contact. | reps: 8 x 20 sec on, 40 sec off | cues: Hot floor, balls of the feet, arms pumping
29. Acceleration starts | Two markers 5 yards apart. Explode from a standstill to the far marker, walk back. | reps: 8 sprints, walk back | cues: Low first step, drive the arms, full recovery every rep

31. 5-10-5 with the ball | Three cones 5 yards apart. Ball at your feet, sprint 5 to one side, 10 to the other, 5 back to the middle. | reps: 6 reps, full recovery | cues: Low hips on the cut, ball close through the turn
