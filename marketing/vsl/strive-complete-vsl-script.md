# Strive Complete Pathway VSL, full script

Coach Yinka to camera. Studio setup. One take per section, cut between.
Target 8 minutes. About 1,150 spoken words. Plain delivery, no hype voice.
Read it like you're talking to one parent across a table.

Rules baked in: no em dashes, no "AI" or "automated", no roster or
scholarship promises, no "your club failed you", no made-up numbers. Alumni
names are allowed here and nowhere public. Give those families a heads-up
before this goes live.

Bracketed lines are B-roll or on-screen text, not spoken.

---

## 0. Cold open (0:00 to 0:25)

[You, to camera. No music yet.]

If your player works hard at practice and you still can't tell if he's actually getting better, this video is for you.

I'm Coach Yinka. I coach players in Northern Virginia, in person, every week. And for the last few months I've been building something for the families I can't see in person, and honestly for the ones I can.

Give me eight minutes. I'm going to show you exactly what it is, who it's for, what it costs, and I'm going to show you a real player's week so you can judge it yourself.

## 1. Who this is for (0:25 to 0:55)

This is for a player who's somewhere between fourteen and eighteen, who has a real goal. College. An academy. Playing at the next level, whatever that level is for him.

And it's for a parent who is done guessing. You've paid for the club. You drive to the practices. You've watched him have a great training session and then disappear in a game. And nobody has ever sat down with you and said, here's where he actually is, here's where he's trying to go, and here's the road between the two.

If that's not you, no hard feelings, this isn't your video.

## 2. The gap (0:55 to 2:00)

[B-roll: a player training alone, cones, a ball at the wall.]

Here's the thing nobody says out loud. The practices aren't the problem. Most club coaches are good at their jobs. The problem is the six days a week your player isn't at practice.

What does he do on a Tuesday night? Be honest. He kicks the ball around. Maybe watches a video. Maybe nothing. And that's not laziness, that's a kid who doesn't know what to work on, with a parent who was never a soccer player and shouldn't have to become one.

So the family does one of three things. You buy more sessions and hope. You buy an app that's the same for every kid on earth. Or you do nothing and tell yourself he'll figure it out.

I've watched all three for years. The ones who make it have something the others don't. Somebody who knows them, watching every week, telling them the next thing.

That's what I built.

## 3. The vacation (2:00 to 3:15)

[Keep it on you. This is the section that sells.]

Picture your player three months from now.

He wakes up on a Monday and his week is already there. Not a generic plan. His week. Built around what he did last week, what he missed, what he's working toward. Four sessions, every drill with a video of exactly how to do it. He does them in the backyard, the garage, the park. You don't say a word.

Every week he gets on a call with me, or with Coach Gary, and we go through it. What we saw. What's next. He hears a coach who's played at a level he's trying to reach talk to him like a player, not like a kid.

Once a month I sit down with his actual game film. Not a highlight reel. The real decisions. The pass he didn't see. The run he should have made. He sees it with my voice over it, and it changes how he plays the next weekend.

And you, every week, get a text from me. What he did. What I saw. What's coming. You're not guessing anymore. You're in it.

Then tryouts come. Or the showcase. Or the trial. And he walks in as the kid who's been doing the work every week for three months with somebody watching. That's the player coaches notice. I can't promise you a roster spot. Nobody honest can. I can promise you he'll be ready for the moment, and you'll know it.

## 4. What it actually is (3:15 to 4:15)

[On-screen text as you say each line. App screen recording behind you.]

It's called the Strive Complete Pathway. Twelve weeks.

Every Monday, a new training week in the app, built by me around your player. Four sessions a week, about forty minutes each, a video for every drill. That's forty-eight sessions over the program.

Every week, a one on one call with me or Coach Gary. Twelve calls.

Every month, a private film breakdown of his real game footage. Three breakdowns.

Every week, a report to you. What got done, what I saw, what's next.

And the app tracks all of it, so he sees his own progress and so do you.

[Show a real week on screen here. Say what you're pointing at.]

This is a real week for one of my players right now. This is Monday's session. This is the video he watches before he does it. This is what he checks off. This is what his parent got on Sunday night.

## 5. Proof (4:15 to 5:30)

[On-screen: names and programs as you say them. Photos if you have permission.]

I want to be careful here, because a lot of people in this space say "countless" and hope you don't ask. So I'll give you names.

Players I've coached have gone on to play Division One at West Virginia, Harvard, Penn State, Syracuse, Marquette, George Mason, William and Mary, College of Charleston, Lafayette, Longwood. Reggie Gainer came through DC United's academy. Tomiwa Adewumi made his professional debut.

Players I've coached have signed with academies at DC United, Inter Miami, Sporting Kansas City, Colorado Rapids, and Galatasaray in Turkey.

And Teymour Mohammed, who trained with me, is at Montpellier in France and has played for the Tunisian national team.

That's not a promise about your kid. That's proof the coaching works when a player does the work.

[Cut to Gary, 20 seconds, to camera.]

GARY: I'm Coach Gary. I played for Armenia's youth national teams and in the under-19 Bundesliga with Fortuna Koln. The overseas path is real, and it's confusing, and most families get bad advice. On these calls I tell you what it actually takes. No agents, no promises, just what I've seen.

[Back to you.]

And the families who started this with me this fall are already in it. Weeks built, calls happening, film coming in.

## 6. The price, and the drop (5:30 to 7:00)

[On-screen numbers as you say them. This is the section you asked for.]

Let's talk about what this costs, and let's do it honestly.

If you wanted this from me in person, four sessions a week, you know the number. My private sessions are eighty dollars. Four a week is three hundred and twenty. A month of that is twelve hundred and eighty dollars.

[On screen: $80 x 4 x 4 = $1,280 a month.]

And that's just the sessions. No weekly call. No film breakdown. No report to you. And I can't physically do four sessions a week with every player who wants it. There aren't enough hours.

The Complete Pathway, four sessions a week, the weekly call, the monthly film breakdown, the weekly report, everything I just showed you, is six hundred dollars a month.

[On screen: $1,280 crossed out. $600 a month.]

Three months. Eighteen hundred total. Or fifteen hundred if you pay in full up front and we're done talking about money.

[On screen: 3 months. $600 a month, or $1,500 paid in full.]

Now, if your player is younger, or the calls and the film aren't what he needs yet, there's a simpler version. Strive Elite. It's the app only. The weekly plan I build, the drill videos, his progress, and the text to you. No calls, no film. That's three hundred and fifty dollars a month, month to month.

[On screen: Strive Elite. $350 a month. App only.]

And if you're watching this before November first, the founding rate for Elite is two hundred forty-nine a month, and it stays two forty-nine for as long as you're in. It never goes up.

[On screen: Before Nov 1: $249 a month, locked for life.]

## 7. The guarantee (7:00 to 7:25)

Here's how I protect you.

On the Complete Pathway, you have fourteen days. If inside two weeks you look at what your player's getting and it's not what I just described, tell me and you get every dollar back. After that, you're in for the three months, and so am I.

On Elite, it's month to month. If it's ever not for you, you tell me and I refund that month the same day. That's always been my policy.

## 8. One ask (7:25 to 8:00)

[On-screen: the form. Arrow to it if the page has one.]

Here's what happens next. There's a short form under this video. Your name, your player's age, what he's working toward. It takes a minute.

Somebody from my team reaches out the same day to set up a call with me. On that call I'm going to ask you three things: where he is right now, where he wants to go, and what's in the way. Then I'll tell you honestly whether this is the right fit, and which one.

I run these calls myself, so I can only take so many families at a time. If you're serious, fill out the form now.

I'll talk to you soon.
