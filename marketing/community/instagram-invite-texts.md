# Instagram community invite texts (Oct 16 2026)

The community is an Instagram group chat named "Strive Elite", players
only, 13 and up. Coach Yinka adds each player from his account. Parents
are not in the chat; they get the weekly text, the progress clip text
and the call reminders from the app and GHL as today. Promise only what
exists: the chat, clips, wins, the app.

## Player, by text or DM (13+)

[First], I'm adding you to the Strive Elite group chat on Instagram
today. That's where you post your clips, see what the other guys are
working on, and where the wins go up. Training stays in the app, the
chat is the locker room around it. Make sure your Instagram is set so I
can add you, or send me your handle. Let's see the work.

## Parent, by text (so nothing lands on a kid's phone without the parent knowing)

Hey [Parent], heads up that I'm adding [First] to the Strive Elite
group chat on Instagram this week. It's the players only, run by me,
and it's where they post clips and wins and see what the others are
working on. Nothing changes for you, your updates keep coming by text.
Let's keep [First] dialed in.

## Parent of a player under 13

Hey [Parent], the Strive Elite players have a group chat on Instagram
now for clips and wins. [First] is under 13 so he stays off it for now,
and everything he needs still comes through the app and your texts.
When he turns 13 I'll add him. Let's keep him going.

## Old and lapsed clients (parent)

Hey [Parent], it's Coach Yinka. The Strive Elite players have a group
chat on Instagram now for clips and wins, and I'd like [First] back
around the group. It's free. If he's on Instagram, send me his handle
and I'll add him. Good to have [First] back with us.

## Rules carried
- "Your player" never "your child". First names only. No sign-off,
  forward-looking close. No emojis, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
- Alumni and D1 names are never in an invite. A D1 alum joins only on
  his own yes.
- No Instagram account is created for a player by us, ever.
