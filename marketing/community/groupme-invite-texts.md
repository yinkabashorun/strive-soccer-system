# GroupMe community invite texts (Oct 16 2026)

The community is one GroupMe group named "Strive Elite", players and
parents together. Coach Yinka adds each person by phone number from the
app; nobody needs to install anything, messages arrive as texts. Admin
only adds, share link off. Under-13s are in through the parent's
number. Promise only what exists: the group, clips, wins, the app.

## Parent (sent before the add, so the first GroupMe text is expected)

Hey [Parent], I'm adding you and [First] to the Strive Elite GroupMe
today. It's the players and families in one place: clips, wins, and
what everyone's working on this week. Training stays in the app, this
is the locker room around it. You'll get a text from GroupMe to join,
no app needed. Let's keep [First] dialed in.

## Player with his own phone

[First], I'm adding you to the Strive Elite GroupMe today. Post your
clips there, see what the other guys are working on, and put your wins
up. You'll get a text from GroupMe to join. Let's see the work.

## Old and lapsed clients (parent)

Hey [Parent], it's Coach Yinka. The Strive players and families have a
GroupMe now for clips and wins, and I'd like [First] back around the
group. It's free. If you're good with it, reply yes and I'll add you
both today. Good to have [First] back with us.

## Rules carried
- "Your player" never "your child". First names only. No sign-off,
  forward-looking close. No emojis, no exclamation points, no em dashes.
- Alumni and D1 names are never in an invite. A D1 alum joins only on
  his own yes.
- Nobody is added without a text first, so the GroupMe invite never
  lands cold.
