# "You're training everything" carousel

Coach Yinka, Nov 11 2026, on the first structure carousel: keep that one
as the shorter version; this one has an opinion, is easy to take in, uses
graphics that make the argument click, and answers the objections. His
verdict on the first one's wording ("the focus", "everything else",
"drill one"): robot copy. So this one is written to the player, in his
voice, starting from a session they already know.

Draft. Nothing is posted until he approves it. Ten 4:5 slides, 1080x1350.
Render one: `node render.mjs 03-two.html 03-two.png`.

## What the copy is doing

- It opens in the reader's own training, not in our program.
- The graphics are the product and the week in real words (his third
  note: "Premium. What is this circle bullshit. Use the right words").
  Slides 3, 4 and 7 are a timetable of blocks named the way he names the
  work (weak foot, first touch, ball mastery, 1v1 skills, passing,
  juggling, speed). Slides 5, 8 and 9 show the app's own session cards
  with real bank drills, their real reps and frames of their clips
  (app/*.jpg). No circles, no charts. Spread over six things, the weak
  foot gets 2. Put on one thing, it gets 8. The dot version of slide 4 he
  first approved is kept as 04-eight-dots.png.
- Progressive overload is one comparison everybody already believes:
  nobody gets stronger lifting the same weight all year.
- His comeback is the proof, in his words.
- The three objections are in the reader's words, as questions, each with
  a short answer and the same dots.
- No label the reader has to learn. No price, no guarantee (not stamped).

## The slides

1. YOU'RE TRAINING EVERYTHING. THAT'S THE PROBLEM. / Why a bit of
   everything keeps you the same player, and what to do about it.
2. YOU KNOW THIS SESSION. / Ten minutes of juggling. Some cone work. A few
   passes off the wall. A couple of shots. / You sweat. You go home feeling
   good. / THREE MONTHS LATER YOU'RE THE SAME PLAYER.
3. YOUR WEAK FOOT GOT 2 DRILLS. / Four sessions. Twelve drills. Six things
   to fix. Each gets two. That's not training your weak foot. That's
   visiting it.
4. SAME TWELVE DRILLS. NOW IT GETS 8. / Two in every session, two weeks
   straight. Sixteen in all. That's enough work to change something. Then
   you move to the next thing.
5. AND IT HAS TO GET HARDER. / Nobody gets stronger lifting the same weight
   all year. Your touch works the same way. Week two is harder than week
   one. Weak foot only. Eyes closed. Then harder again.
6. IT'S HOW I CAME BACK. / Six months out with a hip tear. I couldn't run,
   so for a month I did one thing: a ball and a wall, 45 minutes, every
   day. Then fitness. Then speed. I'd been a bench player. I moved to a
   more competitive team and started.
7. "BUT I'VE GOT MORE THAN ONE THING TO FIX." / Good. You get six. Two
   weeks on each, biggest problem first. That's three months, and every
   one of them gets real work.
8. "SO I ONLY WORK ON ONE THING FOR TWO WEEKS?" / No. One drill in every
   three is the rest of your game, a different part each session. Your
   passing doesn't go cold while your weak foot catches up.
9. "WHAT ABOUT SPEED AND STRENGTH?" / They don't wait their turn. Speed
   work starts the session. Strength ends it. Every session, for as many
   months as you need them.
10. ONE THING AT A TIME. HARDER EVERY WEEK. NOTHING LEFT BEHIND. / That's
    how Coach Gary and I build every player's three months. / DM us
    "ELITE". Tell us the one thing you'd fix first.

## Caption (draft)

A bit of everything is the worst way to train on your own.

Twelve drills a week across six things is two drills each. Two. That is why your weak foot is the same foot it was three months ago.

Put eight of those twelve on one thing for two weeks and it finally gets enough work to change. Make week two harder than week one. Keep one drill a session for the rest of your game so nothing goes cold. Then move to the next thing.

That is how I came back from six months out with a hip tear. It is how Coach Gary and I build every Strive Elite player's three months.

DM ELITE and tell us the one thing you'd fix first.
