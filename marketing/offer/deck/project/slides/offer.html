The answer

One membership. Four parts.

01

The week

A new week every Monday, built around your player. Four sessions in season, six in the off-season, about 40 minutes. Every drill on video. Extra Work when there is more in the tank. Any space.

02

Accountability

A text when the week goes live. Side by Side, the same drill filmed every two weeks, both clips sent to you. Send a Clip, a coach replies in the app. Miss a week, the plan waits. A monthly 1:1 check-in call with your coach.

03

Courses for the weaknesses

Keep It Up, Weak Foot, Strength, Speed, Recruiting, College, Academy. Every course, every member, included. More as we film them.

04

Film Room, every week

Your player's game and training clips, broken down by Coach Yinka and Coach Gary every week. What worked, what to fix, and the drill that fixes it.

Everything in the offer sits under one of these four. Film Room and the monthly check-in call are new to the offer as of Oct 29; their formats are still Coach Yinka's call.
