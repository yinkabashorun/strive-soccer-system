Film Room, every week.

What it is

Your player's game and training clips, broken down by Coach Yinka and Coach Gary every week. What worked, what to fix, and the drill that fixes it.

Why it matters

Practice shows a coach the drill. Film shows the coach the game. Film Room is where the week gets aimed at what actually happens on Saturday.

Clips go in through the app. The breakdown comes back the same week.

Film Room is new to the offer, added Oct 29. The cadence is weekly. Clip count per player, the delivery format and who runs it are still Coach Yinka's call.
